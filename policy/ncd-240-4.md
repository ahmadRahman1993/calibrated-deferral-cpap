# Policy text used by this demo

Retrieval date: Nov 2, 2026.

This file quotes public CMS text and then states how the demo encodes it. It is not a coverage determination.

## Sources

- NCD 240.4, Continuous Positive Airway Pressure (CPAP) Therapy for Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA). Version 3, effective 03/13/2008, implemented 08/04/2008. https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/ncd.aspx?NCDId=226
- LCD L33718, Positive Airway Pressure (PAP) Devices for the Treatment of OSA (DME MAC LCD; revision effective 01/01/2024). https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?LCDId=33718

Several details people associate with NCD 240.4 are spelled out in LCD L33718. The rules engine tags every criterion with its source.

## Quoted text (NCD 240.4)

> An initial 12-week period of CPAP is covered in adult patients with OSA if either of the following criterion using the AHI or RDI are met: a. AHI or RDI greater than or equal to 15 events per hour, or b. AHI or RDI greater than or equal to 5 events and less than or equal to 14 events per hour with documented symptoms of excessive daytime sleepiness, impaired cognition, mood disorders or insomnia, or documented hypertension, ischemic heart disease, or history of stroke.

> A positive diagnosis of OSA for the coverage of CPAP must include a clinical evaluation and a positive: a. attended PSG performed in a sleep laboratory; or b. unattended HST with a Type II home sleep monitoring device; or c. unattended HST with a Type III home sleep monitoring device; or d. unattended HST with a Type IV home sleep monitoring device that measures at least 3 channels.

> If the AHI or RDI is calculated based on less than 2 hours of continuous recorded sleep, the total number of recorded events to calculate the AHI or RDI during sleep testing must be at a minimum the number of events that would have been required in a 2-hour period.

The NCD also refers to a sleep test previously ordered by the beneficiary's treating physician. That phrase is the I4 source recorded for this demo. The surrounding sentence should be re-checked on the NCD page before a claim is made about its exact wording.

## Quoted text (LCD L33718)

> The beneficiary has an in-person clinical evaluation by the treating practitioner prior to the sleep test to assess the beneficiary for obstructive sleep apnea.

> ...no sooner than the 31st day but no later than the 91st day after initiating therapy, the treating practitioner must conduct a clinical re-evaluation and document that the beneficiary is benefiting from PAP therapy.

> Adherence to therapy is defined as use of PAP ≥4 hours per night on 70% of nights during a consecutive thirty (30) day period anytime during the first three (3) months of initial usage.

Continuation rules that depend on the re-evaluation and adherence sentences are out of scope for this version. The sentences are here so the source of that cut is visible.

## How the demo encodes the initial request

| ID | Encoding | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I1 | Adult means age ≥ 18. Missing age is unclear. | NCD 240.4 |
| I2 | In-person evaluation, dated before the sleep test, by the treating practitioner. Telehealth-only refers. Same calendar day, with no time of day, is unclear. | NCD 240.4 + LCD L33718 |
| I3 | Attended PSG, HST II, HST III, or HST IV with at least 3 channels. Oximetry-only does not qualify. | NCD 240.4 |
| I4 | The sleep test was ordered by the treating practitioner. | NCD 240.4 |
| I5 | Tier A: AHI or RDI ≥ 15. Either index qualifies unless `strictIndex` is set. | NCD 240.4 |
| I6 | Tier B: an index from 5 through 14, plus an explicit qualifying symptom or a documented qualifying comorbidity. | NCD 240.4 |
| I7 | Event floor: 30 events for tier A, 10 for tier B. Default (`minEvents: "lcd"`) applies these floors always. The NCD wording (`minEvents: "ncd"`) applies them only when recorded sleep is under 2 hours. | NCD 240.4 + LCD L33718 |

Severity is met when I5 or I6 is met. The other tier stays `not_met` and does not block approval.

An index strictly between 14 and 15 is `POLICY_GAP`. Any study using a 3% oxygen desaturation hypopnea scoring rule is `SCORING_RULE` and refers (Medicare policy requires the 4% rule). Any cross-document conflict refers before the other rules can approve.

The output of the rules engine is `APPROVE` or `REFER`. Criteria that are not met become a referral with reasons.

## Noted and left out

- The NCD text refers to "criteria 1-7 above" without numbering those criteria. Drafting artifact. No rule uses that phrase.
- Sleep-test details that cross-reference NCD 240.4.1 are out of scope.
- Supplier education before use is not part of the initial prior-auth decision in this demo.
